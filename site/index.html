Safety boundary

Evidence is not permission

A green verification proves byte identity. It does not prove truth, completeness, safety, or OWNER acceptance.

  • Local by defaultNo model, cloud service, database, or upload is required.
  • Review before sharingYou inspect the selected context and decide what leaves the machine.
  • Fail closedUnknown state, drift, conflicts, or missing authority stop the action.
